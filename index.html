Why we built Fathom

We got tired of finding out about delays from the customer first.

Our founders spent a decade running warehouse and dispatch operations, refreshing four different carrier portals to answer one question: where is this order right now? Fathom is the tool we wished we had — a single, honest view of every shipment, built for the team that actually has to answer for it.

Everything your dispatch team checks, in one place

Live tracking across every carrier

Fathom connects to your carriers and warehouse systems and lines every shipment up on one timeline, so your team stops switching between five different logins to answer one question.

Exception alerts before they become problems

Fathom flags a shipment the moment it drifts off its expected schedule — not after a customer emails asking where their order is. Your team gets the warning while there's still time to act on it.

Carrier scorecards you can bring to a renewal

See on-time rates, exception frequency, and average delay by carrier over any period, so the next contract conversation is backed by your own numbers instead of a gut feeling.

How it works

  1. 01

    Connect your systems

    Link your carriers, warehouse software, and order management in a single setup pass — no engineering time required.

  2. 02

    See one shared timeline

    Every shipment lands on one dashboard, matched to the order it belongs to, updated as carriers report in.

  3. 03

    Get flagged early

    The moment something drifts off schedule, the right person on your team gets a specific, actionable alert.

  4. 04

    Resolve it quietly

    Fix it before the customer notices, and keep a record of what happened for the next carrier review.
